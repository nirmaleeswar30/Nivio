Downloads

Save movies and episodes to your device and watch them without a connection.

Download something

  1. Open a movie or show and pick the episode you want.
  2. Tap the download button. Nivio finds a downloadable stream and starts saving it.
  3. Follow progress in the Downloads screen in your Library.

Downloads keep going while you use the rest of the app, and the screen stays on while they run so the download isn't interrupted.

Watch while downloading

You don't have to wait for a download to finish. In the Downloads screen, tap Watch while downloading to start playing what's already saved. Finished downloads play straight from your device.

Each download has Pause and Resume buttons. If one fails (you lost connection, for example), tap Retry.

Download settings

In Settings:

SettingWhat it does
Preferred Download AudioThe audio language to keep when a stream offers several
Preferred Download SubtitleThe subtitle language to save alongside the video
Parallel Download ConnectionsHow many connections each download uses. More is usually faster; lower it if a source starts failing.
Note

Some sources only offer streams that can't be saved, such as embedded web players. When that happens, try another server, or stream the title instead.
