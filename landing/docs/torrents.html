What to watch

Torrent streaming

Nivio can play torrents while they download, with no separate torrent client. A built-in engine written in Rust fetches the pieces you're about to watch first, so playback starts within seconds and seeking works.

Turn it on

Torrent streaming is optional. Turn on Enable Torrent Streaming in Settings → Torrents. Torrent results then show up in the provider list as Nivio Torrents.

Note

Torrenting shares the pieces you download with other people. In many countries that's monitored, and sharing copyrighted content may be illegal where you live. Many people use a VPN. You're responsible for what you stream.

While it plays

A stats bar at the top of the player shows download speed and peers, and stays visible while the video is buffering. Torrents with more seeders start faster and stall less, so pick well-seeded results when you have a choice.

Auto-Select Episode File picks the right episode out of a season or batch torrent for you.

Keeping files

By default, Nivio deletes a torrent's files when you stop watching, so they don't fill your storage. Turn on Persist Torrent Files to keep them on disk, which makes rewatching instant.
