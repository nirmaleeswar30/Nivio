What to watch

Anime

Nivio has a dedicated anime hub with several streaming sources, sub and dub audio, filler tagging and one-tap intro skipping. Your progress can sync to AniList, MyAnimeList or Simkl.

Sources and fallback

Nivio streams anime from several sources, tried in this order:

  1. Miruro
  2. Animetsu
  3. AnimeX

If a source doesn't have the episode or fails to load, Nivio moves on to the next by itself, and tries every direct stream before falling back to embedded players. To start from a different source, change Preferred Anime Source in Settings. You can switch servers mid-episode from the player too (see The player).

For anime torrents from Nyaa, see Torrent streaming.

Sub or dub

Set Preferred Anime Audio in Settings to choose subbed (Japanese with subtitles) or dubbed (English) by default. If an episode isn't available in your preference, Nivio plays what the source has.

Filler episodes

Filler episodes are tagged in the episode list, so you can see at a glance which ones you can skip. Turn on Auto Skip Filler in Settings → Playback, and auto-play will pass over them on its way to the next canon episode.

Skipping intros and outros

When an episode's opening and ending times are known, the player shows Skip intro and Skip outro buttons at the right moment. Turn on Auto Skip Intro & Outro to skip them without tapping. Skip times come from community databases (AniSkip and TheIntroDB), so a few episodes won't have them.

New episode alerts

Nivio can check in the background for newly aired episodes of shows and anime you follow and notify you. Turn on Episode Alerts in Settings. Check Now runs the check straight away, and Open New Episodes lists what it found.

Tracking your progress

Connect AniList, MyAnimeList or Simkl and Nivio updates your list as you watch: episodes you finish are marked watched, and new shows land in your planning list. See Sync & integrations.
