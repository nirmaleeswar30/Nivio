Sync & integrations

Nivio works without an account. Sign-ins are optional and each one adds something: cloud sync, list tracking, or Discord status.

Cloud sync with Google

Sign in with Google to keep your watchlist and watch history in sync across all your devices. Progress you make on your phone shows up on your TV and computer, and it all comes back if you reinstall.

On Windows and Linux, sign-in opens in your web browser and returns to Nivio when you're done.

AniList, MyAnimeList and Simkl

Connect your tracking accounts in Settings → Tracking. Nivio then updates your lists as you watch:

  • Finished episodes are marked watched on your list.
  • Titles you add to your watchlist go to your Planning (AniList) or Plan to Watch (MyAnimeList, Simkl) list.
  • Sync Library Now pulls your lists into Nivio's library.

Discord Rich Presence

Show what you're watching in your Discord status, with the title, episode and poster. Turn it on in Settings under Discord Rich Presence.

PlatformHow it connects
Windows and LinuxThrough the Discord desktop app. Keep Discord open and signed in; no extra login. The setting shows whether Discord was found.
AndroidTap the setting and sign in with Discord once. Your status updates even though Discord itself isn't running on the phone.

Episode alerts

Turn on Episode Alerts in Settings and Nivio checks in the background for new episodes of what you're watching, then sends a notification. See Anime for details.
