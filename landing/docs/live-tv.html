What to watch

Live TV & IPTV

The Live TV tab brings together free TV channels, live sports, and any IPTV service you already use.

Free channels

Nivio includes free, legal channel catalogs such as Pluto TV, Samsung TV Plus and Plex Live TV. Browse them by category, search for a channel, and add favourites so they're always at the top.

Live sports

The Sports section lists live and upcoming matches. Tap Watch Live on a match, then pick a stream. If one doesn't play, go back and try another stream for the same match.

Add your own IPTV

If you have an IPTV subscription, add it under Manage Playlists → Add New Playlist. Nivio supports both formats IPTV providers use:

TypeWhat you enterWhat you get
M3U linkThe playlist URL from your providerLive channels
Xtream loginThe server address, username and password from your providerLive channels, plus the provider's Movies and Series libraries

Nivio keeps a copy of large playlists on your device, so they open quickly next time. You can add several playlists and switch between them.

Tip

If your provider gives you both an M3U link and Xtream details, use Xtream: it adds the movie and series libraries, with seasons and episodes.

If a channel won't play

  • Free channels change often. If one stops working, try another channel or refresh.
  • For your own playlist, check that your subscription is active and the details are correct. Many providers limit how many devices can watch at once.
  • Some channels are geo-blocked and only play in certain countries.
